\section{Method}

The exercise considers the Control, Market, Aid, and Bonus versions of DG-KW,
UG, and TG. For Aid and Bonus, the model reads the story first and the
instructions for the corresponding game second, preserving the order used in
the experiment. Each game-frame context is rated against all 30 vignettes under
neutral identifiers. Game labels and the vignette classifications used to
organize the figures are not shown to the model.

For each context, the model assigns an absolute similarity score between 0 and
100 to every vignette. The exercise uses three blinded rating passes. The
analysis then selects the relevant vignette pool: the six DG vignettes for a DG
context, and the combined 24 UG and TG vignettes for either a UG or TG context.
Within rating pass $r$ and context $c$, vignette $v$ in the relevant pool
$\mathcal{P}_{c}$ receives normalized weight

\begin{equation}
    w_{vcr} = \frac{s_{vcr}}{\sum_{j\in\mathcal{P}_{c}}s_{jcr}}.
\end{equation}

The vignette weights therefore sum to one within each pass and context. We
normalize each pass before averaging the three weights, so a pass that uses
systematically higher absolute scores does not receive greater weight.

The vignette classifications are reattached only after rating. M, S, and C
denote Moral, Self-interest, and Cooperation. In UG and TG, the second letter
records whether the receiver chooses C or D. P denotes a personal vignette and
K an anonymous-market vignette. The label before P or K records the structural
source of the vignette (DG, UG, or TG), and Vxx is its neutral identifier.

Each representation class contains its individual vignette bars followed by a
cross-hatched TOTAL bar equal to their sum. DG has three classes, each with two
vignette bars and one total. UG and TG have six classes, each with four vignette
bars---UG-personal, UG-market, TG-personal, and TG-market---and one total.
Individual bars sum to one within a game-frame, and the class-total bars
separately sum to one. The totals are summaries and should not be added again to
the individual bars.

\section{Market versus Control}

For each vignette, Figure~\ref{fig:round6_market_control_similarity} reports
$w_v^{\mathrm{Market}}-w_v^{\mathrm{Control}}$, where the two distributions are
normalized separately using the same game-specific pool. Positive bars indicate
a larger normalized weight in Market. The TOTAL bar is the sum of the vignette
differences in its class. Individual differences sum to zero within each game,
as do the class-total differences.
