\section{Between-subject analysis}

\subsection{Four-category method}

Each participant-frame provides four HP texts, one for each allocation anchor $a\in\{4,6,8,12\}$. Each text is classified as Moral (M), Self-interest (S), Cooperation (C), or No clear justification (N). We estimate the partially pooled multinomial model

\begin{equation}
    \log\frac{\Pr(K_{ia}=k)}{\Pr(K_{ia}=N)}=\alpha_{ka}+b_{ki},
    \qquad k\in\{M,S,C\},
\end{equation}

where the participant-frame effects $b_{ki}$ are Gaussian-shrunk and the penalty is selected by cross-validation. The allocation effects $\alpha_{ka}$ absorb systematic differences across HP anchors; the second stage uses the estimated $b_{Mi}$, $b_{Si}$, and $b_{Ci}$ directly. These effects measure participant deviations in category log odds relative to No clear justification, net of the allocation-anchor effects.

We regress the participant's DG share sent on the three $b_{ki}$ effects, controlling for indicators for LT Control, KW Market, and LT Market, with KW Control omitted. Standard errors are clustered by participant. Confidence intervals refit both stages in 500 participant-cluster bootstrap samples, holding the penalty at its cross-validated value.

\subsection{Moral baseline, excluding No clear justification}

We remove all No clear justification classifications and re-estimate the model on M, S, and C, using Moral as the reference category:

\begin{equation}
    \log\frac{\Pr(K_{ia}=k)}{\Pr(K_{ia}=M)}=\alpha_{ka}+b_{ki},
    \qquad k\in\{S,C\}.
\end{equation}

The analysis retains 3,059 substantive HP classifications from 1,060 participant-frames. The 140 frames containing only No clear classifications are excluded. The estimated $b_{Si}$ and $b_{Ci}$ are participant-frame deviations in the log odds of Self-interest and Cooperation relative to Moral, net of the allocation-anchor effects.

\subsection{Heterogeneity by game and condition}

Using the Moral-baseline sample, we interact $b_{Si}$ and $b_{Ci}$ first with an LT indicator, using KW as the reference game. This regression includes indicators for LT Control, KW Market, and LT Market, with KW Control omitted; hence average allocations may differ across all four game-by-condition cells. For the condition comparison, we retain only KW observations and interact the participant effects with a Market indicator, using KW Control as the reference cell.

\section{Within-subject analysis}

\subsection{Cell-specific HP weights}

The within-subject data contain 1,799 participants and 14,392 HP responses from two pre-choice elicitations. We apply the same frozen M/S/C/N classification prompt, exclude N responses, and estimate separately for each game-by-condition cell $c$:

\begin{equation}
\log\frac{\Pr(K_{ia}=k\mid c)}{\Pr(K_{ia}=M\mid c)}=\alpha^c_{ka}+b^c_{ki},\qquad k\in\{S,C\}.
\end{equation}

The models use 9,467 substantive classifications and retain 3,119 participant-cell observations. Participant-cells with only N responses are excluded. The $b^c_{ki}$ effects are Gaussian-shrunk, with the penalty selected separately by within-cell cross-validation. M/S/C weights are the fitted probabilities averaged over the four allocation anchors.
