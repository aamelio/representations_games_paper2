\section{Between-subject analysis}

\subsection{Four-category method}

Each participant-frame provides four HP texts, one for each allocation anchor $a\in\{4,6,8,12\}$. Each text is classified as Moral (M), Self-interest (S), Cooperation (C), or No clear justification (N). We estimate the partially pooled multinomial model

\begin{equation}
    \log\frac{\Pr(K_{ia}=k)}{\Pr(K_{ia}=N)}=\alpha_{ka}+b_{ki},
    \qquad k\in\{M,S,C\},
\end{equation}

where the participant-frame effects $b_{ki}$ are Gaussian-shrunk and the penalty is selected by cross-validation. The four weights are the fitted probabilities averaged across allocation anchors,

\begin{equation}
    w_{ik}=\frac{1}{4}\sum_a \widehat{\Pr}(K_{ia}=k).
\end{equation}

We regress the participant's DG share sent on $w_{iM}$, $w_{iS}$, and $w_{iC}$, with N omitted, controlling for game-by-condition cells. Standard errors are clustered by participant. Confidence intervals refit both stages in 500 participant-cluster bootstrap samples, holding the penalty at its cross-validated value.

\subsection{Moral baseline, excluding No clear justification}

We remove all No clear justification classifications and re-estimate the model on M, S, and C, using Moral as the reference category:

\begin{equation}
    \log\frac{\Pr(K_{ia}=k)}{\Pr(K_{ia}=M)}=\alpha_{ka}+b_{ki},
    \qquad k\in\{S,C\}.
\end{equation}

The analysis retains 3,059 substantive HP classifications from 1,060 participant-frames. The 140 frames containing only No clear classifications are excluded. The M/S/C weights are fitted probabilities conditional on a substantive classification and sum to one.

\section{Within-subject analysis}

\subsection{Classification and first stage}

The within-subject data contain 1,799 participants and 14,392 HP responses from two pre-choice elicitations. HP descriptions are classified with the same frozen M/S/C/N prompt as in the between-subject analysis. We exclude N responses and estimate

\begin{equation}
 \log\frac{\Pr(K_{iat}=k)}{\Pr(K_{iat}=M)}=\alpha_{ka}+\tau_{k1}\mathrm{Market}_{it}+\tau_{k2}\mathrm{KW}_{it}+\tau_{k3}(\mathrm{Market}\times\mathrm{KW})_{it}+b_{ki}, \qquad k\in\{S,C\}.
\end{equation}

The estimation sample contains 9,467 substantive classifications and 1,732 participants; participants whose HP descriptions are all N are excluded. Allocation, treatment, and Gaussian-shrunk participant effects are estimated jointly; cross-validation selects $\lambda=2$.
To match the between-subject analysis, we express the participant effects as M/S/C prevalence weights. For participant $i$, $w^P_{ik}$ is the fitted probability of category $k$, averaged over the four allocation anchors and evaluated at the common LT-Control reference. The treatment component $\Delta^T_{itk}$ is the probability shift from LT Control to the participant's current Market/KW cell, evaluated at zero participant effect. Both sets of components sum to one (weights) or zero (shifts), so Moral is omitted.

All within-subject results are descriptive, in-sample associations. In particular, $w^P_{ik}$ pools both HP elicitations, so the first-game exercise is not a prospective prediction based only on information observed before the first game.
