## Part 3: Commentary on Rankings

The relative-retrieval rankings completely differ from my Pass 1 absolute similarity ratings.

In Pass 1, I rated each game as identically similar to both Story 1 and Story 2 (e.g., Game A was 95 for both; Game D was 10 for both). This is because the absolute similarity task evaluated pure, underlying decision architecture (such as whether the recipient had a continuous choice or veto power), which is structurally identical across both stories.

However, human memory retrieval is highly cue-dependent and driven by surface features, semantics, and context rather than abstract game theory. Therefore, in a retrieval context, Games A and B strongly cue the "windfall/unearned" surface framing of Story 2, while Games C and D cue the "joint effort/entitlement/collaboration" themes of Story 1. This psychological reliance on thematic resonance completely breaks the structural ties seen in Pass 1.
