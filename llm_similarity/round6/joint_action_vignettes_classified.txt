# Joint-action vignettes with classifications

# DG

## DG-M-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: C-NA

Anna receives a set of meal vouchers and must decide how many to keep and how many to give to Marco. She says that, since both of them have the same need for meals, the fair choice is to divide the vouchers equally.

## DG-M-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: C-NA

Firm A received a transaction bonus from an online platform for completing an order with the platform. The manager of firm A splits the bonus with firm B, its input supplier. It is fair to do so, she thinks.



## DG-S-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: D-NA

David receives some travel credits from a travel agency. He thinks whether to give some to his employee, Laura, but decides to keep them all to himself. The credits were given to him and he values them quite a bit.

## DG-S-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: D-NA

An online marketplace assigns a sales rebate to a selling firm and allows it to transfer any part of the rebate to the buyer. The seller's manager decides to keep all the rebate, to maximize her firm's earnings.

## DG-C-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Cooperation
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: C-NA

Giovanni receives the reward from a project he completed with Paola and decides how much of it to give her. Giovanni gives Paola a substantial share so that both benefit from the returns of their joint work.



## DG-C-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Cooperation
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: C-NA

Firms A and B are matched on an online platform for a one-time project. The platform pays the project's reward to Firm A, which alone decides how much of it to transfer to Firm B. Firm A transfers a substantial share to B so that both can benefit from the return of their joint work.

# UG

## UG-M-CC-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

A local theater offers some concert credits to two friends, Elena and Paolo, who must jointly decide whether to accept the credits. Elena proposes a 50:50 split arguing it's the fair outcome. Paolo accepts the offer, so they obtain the credits.

## UG-M-CC-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

An online platform gives a bonus to Firm A subject on A's input supplier, Firm B, accepting the giveaway. Firm A assesses that an equal split is fair, and B accepts it. The bonus is thus distributed to the firms.

## UG-M-CD-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: D
* Joint action: C-D

A local theater offers some concert credits to two friends, Elena and Paolo, who must jointly decide whether to accept the credits. Elena proposes a 50:50 split arguing it's the fair outcome. Paolo does not accept the offer, so the credits are lost.

## UG-M-CD-K

* Sender category: Moral

* Joint action: C-D

An online platform gives a bonus to Firm A subject on A's input supplier, Firm B, accepting the giveaway. Firm A assesses that an equal split is fair, but B does not accept it. Thus, the bonus is lost.

## UG-S-DC-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: D-C

Luca and Giulia can receive a set of event credits if they agree on a division. Luca values the credit very much, and proposes to keep most to for himself leaving just a few to Giulia, to make sure she accepts. Giulia accepts the proposal, so the credits are paid.

## UG-S-DC-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: D-C

Firm A must set the price for its product for a final good producer, Firm B. The firm sets a high price, leaving a small but positive margin for B. Firm B accepts, because some margin is better than none.

## UG-C-CC-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Cooperation
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

Chiara and Stefano have access to a set of production credits, but only if they agree on a division. Chiara argues that this is a great profit opportunity for both, and proposes to reap it with an equal split with Stefano. Stefano accepts and the credits are distributed.

## UG-C-CC-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Cooperation
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

Two firms can undertake a joint project, but first they need to agree on how to split future profits. One firm argues that this is a terrific opportunity for both, so proposes an equal split. The other firm accepts, and they undertake the project and split the profits.

## UG-C-CD-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Cooperation
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: D
* Joint action: C-D

Chiara and Stefano have access to a set of production credits, but only if they agree on a division. Chiara argues that this is a great profit opportunity for both, and proposes to reap it with an equal split with Stefano. Stefano rejects, deeming his share too low. The credits are lost.

## UG-C-CD-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Cooperation
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: D

# TG

## TG-M-CC-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

Miriam has a savings account with some dollars and should decide how many to send to Roberto, who invests the dollars at a 300% return and then decides how much to return to Miriam. Miriam says that splitting her savings account equally is fair, so she sends half to Roberto. Roberto returns a fair share of his investment output, so both people gain from the investment.

## TG-M-CC-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Sender allocation: keeps 3, gives 3
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

Firm A receives a payment from an online platform. It must now decide how much to send to Firm B, which can reinvest at a 300% return. Firm A says that sending half of the initial payment is fair. Firm B invests the money and returns a sizable share to Firm A, so both firms gain from the investment.

## TG-S-DC-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Sender allocation: sends little
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: D-C

Nicola can send part of his funds to Beatrice for a project that generates 300% returns, after which Beatrice chooses how much to pay back. Nicola is worried that Beatrice might pay him nothing, so, to protect his own payoff, he sends only a small amount. Beatrice generates the extra returns and sends a payment back to Nicola, so they both gain from the investment.
