# Vignette construction scheme

The final pool contains 22 narratives: 6 DG, 8 UG, and 8 TG. All narratives are
written around the payoff and action structure of their target game while varying
the intended representation systematically.

## Balanced construction

- DG: Moral, Self-interest, and Cooperation, each represented once in a personal
  setting and once in an anonymous-market setting (3 x 2 = 6).
- UG: M-C, S-C, C-C, and C-D, each represented in the same two settings
  (4 x 2 = 8).
- TG: M-C, S-D, C-C, and C-D, each represented in the same two settings
  (4 x 2 = 8).

For UG and TG, the first component identifies the sender representation and the
second the receiver action. The paired personal/anonymous-market versions are
intended to vary surface setting without changing the underlying game structure,
sender representation, or receiver response.

## Final DG-C edit

The final revision removed incidental statements that the sender was happy or
pleased and references emphasizing the sender's remaining payoff from both DG-C
narratives. The complementary-contribution, joint-value, and sharing rationale was
preserved. Exact replacements are recorded in `dg_c_wording_changes.json`, and both
edited texts were reclassified as Cooperation.
